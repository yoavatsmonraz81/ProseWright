- SETTING: Lantern Quay, a small walled harbour on a reef-bound coast, autumn 1792. A lighthouse on the mole, a weigh-house, a harbour office, an Admiralty survey office up the coast at Kettering Point.
- FORM: A short roleplay log. Wren (the player) acts; a third-person narrator voices the world and everyone else.
- CAST: Wren Calloway (apprentice chartmaker), Ilse Calloway (her older sister, harbour clerk), Captain Amos Varga (master of the brig Gull's Due).
- PREMISE: The reef passages are secret; only naval pilots hold charts of them. Charts sealed with the survey office's broken-north compass rose are worth a fortune to privateers.
