WORLD MECHANICS

- Sound carries over still water at night; voices on the quay can be heard from the harbour office window, but not words.
- The lighthouse beam sweeps every twelve seconds; anything on the quay is lit for a moment and then dark again.
- The weigh-house closes at sunset; nothing leaves the quay at night without the harbourmaster's chit.
- The harbourmaster lives in the customs house on the mole. The bell signal (three strokes) brings him and the barracks guard within minutes.
